## What these results establish

The per-start correction detects the previously identified score bias without using an oracle, but its actual learning result does not clear the declared minimum over random replay. This does not refute the original known-policy diagnostic or prove equivalence/worseness.

The changing-mask result is strongly reversed relative to the registered myopic-advantage prediction. That direction is reported as observed, not retrospectively relabeled confirmatory support. The adapter holds passive traces fixed and does not rerun the original online learners. H3 is one specific successful control comparison; it does not rescue H2 or prove a general MONA repair.

RR coverage restoration corrects a graph-information artifact, but the declared useful behavior improvement was not established. The harm non-inferiority component passes only because both hazard policies are equally harmful. Future studies need a feasibility condition showing nontrivial protection before claiming it was preserved.

Sample sizes and compute were fixed bounded choices, not prospectively power-validated large trials. Calibration established engineering/runtime feasibility, not guaranteed power. Seed-level intervals retain their stated assumptions, selected toy-task scope and finite-cohort degeneracy limits. Separate audits are implementation/evidence checks by the same agent, not independent researcher reproduction.
